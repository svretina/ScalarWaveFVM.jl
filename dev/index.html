ScalarWaveFVM.Diagnostics.semidiscrete_energy_terms — Method
semidiscrete_energy_terms(Q, source, params, cells)

Return the exact semidiscrete energy decomposition on a contiguous, interior range of finite-volume cells. boundary_flux_left and boundary_flux_right are positive outwards from the selected control volume. With g(Q)=QᵀHAQ/2 and numerical face flux F̂, they are g(Q_a)-Q_aᵀHF̂_{a-1/2} and Q_bᵀHF̂_{b+1/2}-g(Q_b). This gives rate = source_power - boundary_flux - dissipation + reconstruction_residual.

ScalarWaveFVM.Diagnostics.semidiscrete_energy_terms — Method
semidiscrete_energy_terms(Q, source, params)

Return the exact periodic semidiscrete energy decomposition for the MUSCL operator used by ODE.muscl_optimized!:

rate = source_power - dissipation + reconstruction_residual.

The energy is h/2 Σ(Π²/c² + Ψ²). Godunov and Rusanov both have wave-speed dissipation c/2 ΔQᵀHΔQ; the residual measures the exact change caused by feeding reconstructed rather than cell-average states to the numerical flux.

ScalarWaveFVM.ScalarField.Φs — Method
Φs(x, z, v, q, τ)

Retarded-matched 1+1 puncture for the positive-source convention □Φ = q δ(x-z)/γ, with r = x-z and accumulated proper time τ:

Φs = (qγ/2) * (|r| + v*r) - (q/2) * τ Πs = -(qγ/2) * (1 + v*sign(r)) Ψs = +(qγ/2) * (sign(r) + v)

The first-order fields equal the one-sided local retarded fields. Velocity derivatives hold r and τ fixed, as required by the remainder equations.
